# About Bisma

Bisma is a full-stack developer and AI engineer with 5+ years of experience building products that people enjoy using. She combines strong engineering foundations with a product mindset, working at the intersection of great engineering and thoughtful UX.

Bisma graduated with a Computer Science degree from MIT in 2019. She is based in San Francisco, CA, and is open to remote work opportunities.

## Background & Journey

Bisma started her career with vanilla JavaScript and progressively mastered React, TypeScript, Edge runtimes, and AI-powered interfaces. Her journey spans frontend, backend, DevOps, and cutting-edge AI/ML systems.

She has shipped features used by hundreds of thousands of users and contributed to open-source tooling with thousands of GitHub stars. Outside of coding, Bisma is a hobbyist game developer using Unity and Godot, an espresso enthusiast (4 cups a day), and an occasional conference speaker.
